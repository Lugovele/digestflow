You are deciding the controlling angle for a LinkedIn post before a post brief is written.

You are given:
- a topic and digest title;
- an optional source_evidence_pack extracted from source text;
- an optional author_take that captures the intended human position;
- digest articles as fallback/context.

TASK:

Choose one narrow editorial angle for the post.

This is an angle decision, not a post and not a full post brief.

ANGLE RULES:

* Do not write the final post.
* Do not write a post brief.
* Do not summarize articles.
* Choose one controlling angle only.
* If `author_take.core_opinion` is present, it must be the primary source of `controlling_angle`.
* Prefer a controlling angle built from `author_take.core_opinion`, `author_take.reader_mistake`, `author_take.reader_check`, and `author_take.practical_point`.
* `controlling_angle` must be phrased as a human/editorial claim, not as a source concept, source term, article title, or branded framework.
* A source term may become the controlling angle only if `author_take.core_opinion` explicitly names that source term as central.
* Use source evidence as support, not permission to choose a louder source term.
* Identify source terms that may support the angle.
* Identify source terms or topics that must not become the main angle.
* If a source term is catchy, branded, or broad, and `author_take` does not explicitly choose it, place it in `do_not_make_main_angle`.
* `allowed_supporting_terms` must contain only terms that can support the author_take without hijacking it.
* `allowed_supporting_terms` must not include a term that is also the main topic of `controlling_angle`.
* `do_not_make_main_angle` should include adjacent source terms/topics that could pull the post away from the author_take.
* `angle_to_avoid` should clearly describe the generic or source-term-led angle to avoid.
* Do not make `Brand Lag`, outdated perceptions, professional image, authority, trust, or reputation the main angle unless `author_take` explicitly selects that framing.
* Do not let a catchy source term override the human position.
* Use source evidence only to support or constrain the angle.
* Do not invent facts, metrics, cases, examples, names, personal experience, or first-person claims.
* If the evidence is thin, choose the safest narrow angle rather than a broad generic branding angle.
* Avoid broad generic angles about authenticity, visibility, storytelling, engagement, trust, or elevating a brand.

Return valid JSON only.
Return exactly this JSON shape:
{{
  "controlling_angle": "string",
  "angle_source": "author_take | source_evidence | fallback",
  "why_this_angle": "string",
  "allowed_supporting_terms": ["string"],

  "angle_to_avoid": "string"
}}
